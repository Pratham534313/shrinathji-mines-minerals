About Shreenathji Mines & Minerals

Built Around Reliability

Shreenathji Mines and Minerals is a mineral supply business based in Bhuj, Gujarat. We work with industrial buyers who need materials that meet their specification, batch after batch.

Our approach rests on careful sourcing, attention to material quality and clear communication on requirements, quantities and schedules. We aim for dependable supply that industrial users can plan around, and we build our relationships with customers for the long term.

Consistency Matters.

For an industrial buyer, a mineral is only as useful as it is repeatable. Process settings, formulations and finished-product quality all depend on material that arrives the same way each time.

  • Consistent material qualityMaterial supplied to an agreed specification, shipment after shipment.
  • Proper sourcingKnowing where material comes from and how it is handled.
  • Clear specificationsRequirements defined and confirmed before supply begins.
  • Reliable supplyQuantities and schedules that buyers can plan around.
  • Professional communicationPrompt, precise responses to technical and commercial queries.
